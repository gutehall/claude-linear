# SIT — Stop, Inspect, Think

This command warrants more reasoning than a routine turn, regardless of the session's default effort level — a self-audit that goes through the motions is worse than no self-audit at all. Take the extra time to genuinely reconsider, not just restate what already happened.

Load the **sit** skill and follow its protocol (`sit.md` in that skill's folder) in full: STOP → INSPECT → THINK → DECIDE. Do not skip steps, do not write optimistic filler — this is a genuine reassessment, not a summary of what was done.

End with one of the four outcomes: Continue, Correct course, Ask the user, or Stop and report.
